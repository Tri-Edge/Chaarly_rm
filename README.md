## About

I’m a developer and founder specializing in transforming ideas into useful, scalable digital products.

- **Full-stack development** — building end-to-end web applications.
- **Cross-platform applications** — web, mobile, and desktop apps for Android, iOS, Windows, and Linux.
- **SaaS platforms & product engineering** — scalable web products with authentication, dashboards, APIs, subscriptions, and multi-tenant architecture.
- **AI-assisted engineering** — AI stacks, coding agents, MCP integrations, and modular monorepos to ship MVPs faster, Hybrid Setup API and local optimisation.
- **Creative technology** — combining software, 3D workflows, and AI-assisted creation.
- **Product mindset** — prioritizing usability, maintainability, and long-term scalability.


My objective is simple: build digital products that are technically solid, thoughtfully designed, and valuable to their users.

## Current focus: 
shipping MVPs faster, hardening SaaS backends for reliability and observability, and growing the Jarvis ecosystem with plugins and skills.

- **AI Assistants** — a modular, Jarvis-like assistant for both everyday personal use and business management. It supports automation, research, productivity, voice control, and agent workflows, with a local-first LLM approach for privacy and security.


- **Web & Motion Agency** — currently developing an upcoming agency specializing in website development, motion design, and digital experiences for brands and businesses.

- **Creator SaaS** — building a soon-to-launch SaaS platform designed for artists and content creators.
